## Research

| **[Affording Generative AI Across Countries](https://github.com/Noah-TaeHwan/affording-generative-ai)** | Working paper (2026) on how far local prices of consumer AI plans adjust to national income. Audits App Store prices for nine ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini plans in 170 storefronts, compares the cost of a \$20 plan with income in 201 economies, and contrasts two measures of AI use. English and Korean versions; [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7535900) · replication package on [Zenodo](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23006365). |
